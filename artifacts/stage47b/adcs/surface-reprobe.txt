=== 4. CORRECTION TO PRIOR EVIDENCE ===
docs/stage46i-certification.md and artifacts/stage47/baseline/
adcs-surface-probe.txt record two findings that this probe contradicts.
They are corrected here; the original files are NOT edited or deleted.

  WRONG (Stage 46i):  'pKICertificateTemplate classSchema in schema -> absent'
  ACTUAL:              the classSchema IS present:
                       cn=PKI-Certificate-Template, lDAPDisplayName=pKICertificateTemplate

  INCOMPLETE (Stage 46i): only CN=Public Key Services was listed as present.
  ACTUAL:                 CN=Enrollment Services and CN=Certificate Templates
                           are ALSO present under it.

  This strengthens rather than weakens the conclusion. Samba ships the AD
  PKI schema class and the PKI container skeleton, but the containers are
  EMPTY and the classes pKIEnrollmentService, certificationAuthority and
  pKIExtendedKeyUsage are absent. A schema class and an empty container are
  not a CA: there is no CA signing key, no CA certificate, no
  certificate database, no templates to analyse, and no MS-WCCE endpoint.
  The absence is a runtime/servicing absence, not a schema absence, which
  is precisely why this cannot be substituted for Windows AD CS.
